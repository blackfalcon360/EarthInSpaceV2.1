# Earth in Space 🌍

Where is Earth right now? Zoom out step by step — all calculated on the phone (no internet needed; location only for the compass).

- **☀️ Solar:** top view of the Solar System with the real positions of the planets today. Earth's path so far this year is drawn in green, with the equinoxes, solstices, perihelion (P) and aphelion (A). Live Earth stats: distance from the Sun, speed, sunlight travel time, kilometres travelled since 1 January, next equinox/solstice. Date slider (±1 year) + −30 d / −1 d / Now / +1 d / +30 d. Tap any planet or the Sun.
- **⭐ Stars:** the Milky Way disk seen from above with the Sun in the centre and 51 stars, clusters and a nebula at their real galactic positions. Zoom from 15 to 2,000 light-years. Every label says "visible" (seen with your own eyes) or "telescope only". Tap a star.
- **🌌 Galaxy:** schematic Milky Way with the arm names written along the arms (Sagittarius, Perseus, Scutum–Centaurus, Norma–Outer, and the Orion Spur), the Sun 26,700 light-years from the centre, and its orbit. Zoom in to the Sun's neighbourhood.
- **🔭 Address:** Earth → Moon → Solar System → heliosphere → Oort Cloud → Local Cloud → Local Bubble → Orion Spur → Milky Way → Local Group → Virgo → Laniakea → Observable Universe, with light-crossing times, plus all the speeds you are moving at.

- **🧭 Compass button (top right):** turns a compass dial on/off. Hold the phone flat: it shows which way, in your real sky, are the Sun, the direction Earth is travelling, the galactic centre, the Sun's path round the galaxy, the motion against the Big Bang glow, and Polaris (Earth's axis). Needs location (asked only when you turn the compass on).

Portrait only. Package: `EarthInSpace.blackfalcon.jan`. Brought to you By: Black Falcon 🦅
